06.08.26🇱🇦 fr. 19
Listening to Cher — Believe

Today I continued my journey south, destination Cambodia. To get there I rented this fast boat, just me and the captain on board. Today's leg only required a couple of hours of travel, which turned out to be very pleasant except for the last 20 minutes. We ran into a storm, the river became choppy and the boat kept drifting. When we tried to dock at the pier closest to my hotel, as we approached the boat's engine died several times and the boat kept spinning in circles at the mercy of the current. I was really scared, but that's the beauty of this trip: tomorrow, destination Wat Phou and the Bolaven Plateau!
